# Repository instructions

Read `docs/SPEC.md` and `prompts/KICKOFF.md` before making changes. Treat the specification as requirements, not evidence that external integrations are supported. Validate Codex and Dot integration assumptions experimentally. Preserve direct native Codex and Dot workflows; the scheduler is additive and optional. Start with dummy projects only. Do not connect or modify real projects without explicit authorization. Keep a testable audit trail and do not silently change scope or bypass safety controls.
