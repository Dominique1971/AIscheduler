PROPOSED DESIGN

Harnesses are created locally from a plan file, not from this Site, which stays read-only. The planned flow is: pick a plan, choose a template, assign models to roles, set gates and the runner schedule, then review the generated file.

Example harness.yaml
project: example-project
harness: software
roles:
  planner:    anthropic/opus
  builder:    openai/codex-latest
  judge:      anthropic/fable     # different family than the builder
  artist:     local/flux3         # or local/flux2
gates:
  accept_score: 4.5
  attempts_per_stage_per_day: 2
  daily_cap_usd: 10
execution:
  runner: cmd
  mailbox: state/harness/inbox
  watchdog_minutes: 15
  default_time_box_minutes: 45
  max_repair_rounds: 2
  min_queued_tasks: 5
  review: read_only_second_session
